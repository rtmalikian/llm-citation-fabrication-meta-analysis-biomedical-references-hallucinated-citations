# Robustness pass version notes, Review 2 (23 October 2026)

For the Zenodo and medRxiv version uploads and the JMLA submission. The corrected version supersedes the first version (22 October 2026) on every figure. Platform handling stays with the parent agent: nothing was uploaded to Zenodo or medRxiv by this pass.

## What the corrected version is

A robustness pass, directed by the author after an audit disclosed three step-downs from the full Cochrane-level process in the first version, closed all three: (1) the search was expanded (full OpenAlex retrieval attempted and blocked after retries; arXiv and Semantic Scholar retried by two routes each and blocked; systematic citation chasing added 489 records); (2) every included study received a second, fully independent extraction, compared field by field against the first, with every discrepancy settled by re-reading the source; (3) all screening disagreements (913 records) and all risk of bias domain disagreements were re-adjudicated by reading, replacing the coded screening rule and the more-cautious-wins risk of bias rule. One duplicate study was found and merged (Jedrzejczak 2024 is the preprint of Pastucha 2026). Included generation studies rose from 25 to 63; the audit stratum is unchanged.

## Headline numbers, first version to corrected version

- Completely fabricated: 18.6% (95% CI 12.8 to 26.3; 51 arms; 12,828 refs) to 19.4% (14.9 to 24.8; 113 arms; 22,461 refs)
- Any major inaccuracy: 36.0% (19.8 to 56.3; 9 arms, 5 studies) to 16.5% (5.7 to 39.2; 20 arms, 14 studies). The first version figure rested on counts that settlement found did not map to the printed definitions; the corrected manuscript flags it as a figure not to quote.
- Fully accurate: 29.4% (19.5 to 41.7; 42 arms) to 37.0% (27.2 to 47.9; 58 arms)
- Retrieval contrast: 5.0% vs 25.1% fabricated (5 retrieval arms) to 5.8% vs 31.0% (26 retrieval arms)
- Risk of bias overall (63 studies): high 30, some concerns 33, low 0 (first version, 25 studies: high 15, some concerns 10, low 0, under the retired cautious rule)
- GRADE: very low for all three critical outcomes, unchanged
