# Extraction notes — Extractor A, batch 2 (NEW included studies, robustness pass, 23 Oct 2026)

Independent extraction. One row per model arm (model × retrieval condition); all rows `include_generation`.
Conventions: counts exactly as printed unless flagged DERIVED under mapping rule 6 (percentage × stated
denominator gives a unique whole count, cross-checked against the study's other category percentages
summing to the denominator). Blank = not extractable / not mapped, never zero. No study in this batch
reported a separable minor-error count or a bad-DOI count, so `n_minor_error` and `n_bad_doi` are blank
throughout, and `n_major_inaccuracy_incl_fabricated` is blank throughout (reasons per study below).

## R2314 — Rao_2026 (JMIR Medical Informatics, GPT-4, scars/keloids patient education, China)
- Extractable: 1 arm. 3,250 references generated across 354 Reddit questions; **fabricated = 383**
  ("hallucinated", 11.8%) and 2,867 "real" (88.2%). Source: Abstract and Results §Reference Evaluation.
- Mapped: fabricated — exact. The study's hallucination test (verified via direct links or Google search;
  real = actual website or academic paper) matches the review's "no matching publication exists".
- Did NOT map: fully accurate — "real" is an existence test only; no check that all bibliographic fields
  were correct, so it is broader than strict fully accurate. Major inaccuracy — no category beyond the
  real/hallucinated binary. 
- Caveat: the 3,250 denominator covers websites as well as academic papers. The parallel website-question
  set (38 unique questions) has its reference results only in Multimedia Appendix 6 / supplements, which
  are not in the full text supplied, so no second arm could be extracted.

## R2352 — Zhou_2026 (Graefe's Arch Clin Exp Ophthalmol, SMILE surgery review generation, China)
- Extractable: 4 arms (DeepSeek R1, Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT-4, Grok-3), 9 topics, 36 reviews.
  Table 2 prints total references per model and category **percentages only** (True and relevant /
  True but not relevant / Fictitious).
- DERIVATIONS (rule 6, all unique and cross-validated by the three categories summing to N):
  - DeepSeek R1: N=200; fictitious 40.5% = **81** exactly (96 + 23 + 81 = 200).
  - Gemini 2.5 Pro: N=96; fictitious 45.8% → **44** (44/96 = 45.83%; 48 + 4 + 44 = 96).
  - GPT-4: N=95; fictitious 49.5% → **47** (47/95 = 49.47%; 38 + 10 + 47 = 95).
  - Grok-3: N=86; fictitious 25.6% → **22** (22/86 = 25.58%; 55 + 9 + 22 = 86).
- Mapped: fabricated — exact ("Fictitious (hallucination)", verified by manual retrieval in PubMed/Google Scholar).
- Did NOT map: fully accurate — "True and relevant" requires existence + relevance, not correctness of all
  bibliographic fields. Major inaccuracy — "True but not relevant" is a relevance judgement, not defined as
  misattribution or a retrieval-preventing error, so fictitious + not-relevant is not an exact sum for major.
- Discrepancies flagged (not corrected): Results text gives Grok fictitious as 25.5% vs Table 2's 25.6%
  (Table 2 used); Discussion says "more than half of the references generated by GPT were fabricated"
  vs Table 2's 49.5%. Retrieval coded "none" for all arms: the study set no browsing/RAG condition
  (free/basic interfaces); the Discussion's claim that Grok's design emphasises RAG is the authors'
  speculation about the product, not a study condition.

## R2551 — Liu_2026 (BMC Oral Health, OMFS licensing-exam questions, China) — NO OUTCOME MAPS
- Printed (Table 5, genuine / "fabricated"): Qwen3 43/26 of 69; DeepSeek-R1-0528 37/29 of 66;
  ChatGPT o3-pro 32/22 of 54; Gemini 2.5 pro 29/21 of 50; Claude 4.0 39/24 of 63. `refs_generated`
  carries these denominators; all outcome columns are blank.
- Why nothing maps: the Methods define the operative classification — "verified" = located in the
  databases **and pertinent to the query content**; "unverified" = **unavailable or irrelevant**.
  Table 5's "Fabricated" is the unverified count, so it includes real-but-irrelevant references and is
  **broader than the review's completely fabricated** (rule 3, not exact), despite the Results narrative
  calling the remainder "fabricated or non-existent". Symmetrically, "Genuine" (existence + pertinence,
  no bibliographic-field check) does not map to strict fully accurate. Major/minor/bad DOI not reported.
- This is the batch's clearest definition-mismatch case; the printed counts above are preserved here for
  narrative synthesis under the study's own labels.

## R5403 — Schumacher_2024 (Int J Retina Vitreous, uveitis case Q&A, Switzerland/UK)
- Extractable: 1 arm (paid ChatGPT-4o, printed "ChatGPT 4.o"). References pooled across all 3 attempts;
  no per-attempt reference split is printed, so attempts are not separate arms.
- Counts as printed (Abstract; Results): of 52 references — **fully accurate = 22** ("accurate and
  correctly cited"), **fabricated = 22** ("could not be located in any of the searched databases",
  7 databases searched), remaining 8 exist but were "either misinterpreted or incorrectly cited".
- Mapped: fabricated — exact; fully accurate (strict) — exact.
- Did NOT map: major inaccuracy — the 8 mix misinterpretation (misattribution) with incorrect citation
  of unstated severity, so 22 + 8 is not an exact sum for major; minor error likewise not separable.

## R5556 — Sina_2024 (OTO Open, COVID-19 olfactory dysfunction patient education, USA)
- Extractable: 1 arm only. References were requested in just one of the four prompt conditions
  ("prompting for references"), and the paper states all unique references arose in that thread; the
  other three prompt conditions produced no unique references, so they contribute no arm.
- Counts: **fabricated = 1 of 12 unique citations** ("falsified"; Discussion: "for a nonexistent
  publication", 8.3%); 11/12 "legitimate".
- Mapped: fabricated — exact.
- Did NOT map: fully accurate — "legitimate" = has a DOI and is findable online (existence test only).
  Major/minor/bad DOI not reported.
- Arithmetic problem flagged (not corrected): the text states both "27 total references" and
  "Twelve (44.4%) of the 24 total references provided were unique" — 27 ≠ 24, and 12/27 = 44.4%
  fits the 27 figure. The verified denominator used is the 12 unique citations, the only set with an
  outcome classification.

  real = 40; ChatGPT-4o — 62 references, **fake = 50**, real = 12.

## R6331 — Ok_2026 (Int J Impotence Research, penile prosthesis review generation, Turkey/Belgium)
- Extractable: 1 arm (ChatGPT-5, public web interface, no fixed API snapshot). 28 citations verified
  against PubMed, Google Scholar, Crossref and journal websites by two independent reviewers.
- DERIVATIONS (rule 6): the paper prints percentages only — fully accurate 35.7% of 28 → **10**
  (10/28 = 35.71%, unique whole count); entirely fabricated ~17.9% of 28 → **5** (5/28 = 17.86%,
  unique); partially inaccurate = 13 by subtraction (46.4%).
- Mapped: fabricated — exact (no corresponding publication in major databases/journal archives, details
  match no existing article); fully accurate (strict) — exact (real publication, all major elements —
  title, authorship, journal, year, DOI/identifier — correct; the study's category definitions are
  printed verbatim in Methods and match the review definitions).
- Did NOT map: major / minor — "partially inaccurate" (real, identifiable, ≥1 element wrong) explicitly
  includes minor metadata errors alongside identity-level errors, with no split. Bad DOI sits inside
  partially inaccurate and is not counted separately.
- Ambiguity flagged: Methods predefine exactly 5 references per section, but with a 7-section outline
  that implies 35 requested while only 28 citations were generated; total requested is not printed
  unambiguously, so `refs_requested` is blank.

## R6555 — Safrai_2024 (J Assist Reprod Genet, fertility preservation review generation, USA/Israel)
- Extractable: 1 arm (ChatGPT-4). 25 references requested (5 per section × 5 sections) and 25 generated.
- Counts as printed (Abstract; Results §Reference validation): **fully accurate = 9** (36%; existing,
  all parameters correct — title, journal, authors and DOI verified where provided);
  **fabricated = 4** (16%; completely fictitious — worked example: no article with that title exists and
  its DOI resolves to a different article); remaining 12 (48%) had a correct title but other fictitious
  parameters (e.g. authors who did not write the paper).
- Mapped: fabricated — exact; fully accurate (strict) — exact.
- Did NOT map: major / minor — the 12 "correct title, other errors" are not split by severity, so
  4 + 12 is not an exact sum for major inaccuracy. Bad DOI — 20/25 references carried a DOI, but no
  count of non-resolving or wrong DOIs is printed (only the single worked example), so not extractable.
- Verifier independence coded "no": both verifiers are the study authors, and one of them (MS) also
  created the manuscript by prompting ChatGPT-4.
