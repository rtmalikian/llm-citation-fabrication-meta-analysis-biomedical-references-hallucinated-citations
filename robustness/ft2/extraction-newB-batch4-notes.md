## Per-record notes

- **R2620 Ettien_2026** (JMLA; 6 AI chat tools vs PubMed for literature searches). 6 arms. Hallucinated = could not verify / no matching source (PubMed, journal, web search) → exact for fabricated. "Real" pooled correct + partially correct, so no strict fully accurate count exists for any arm. Counts are Table 1 totals across both prompts, including duplicates and cross-prompter repetitions. Model versions were reported inconsistently by participants, so model_version is descriptive and model_year is blank. **Arithmetic flag:** narrative reports Gemini 97/153 hallucinated; Table 1 cells sum to 103/159. Table 1 used (it reconciles exactly for the other five tools). No funding or conflicts statements appear in the article text.
- **R2931 Idan_2025** (Sci Rep; GPT-4o / Gemini 2.0 / Claude Sonnet 3.5 vs human researchers, complex clinical queries). Fabricated = source does not exist (exact). Major = fabricated + unfaithful (real source, citation not faithful to its content = misattribution), exact by sum; unfaithful numerators reconcile with the pooled Table 1 figure (26). "Faithful" is content faithfulness, not bibliographic accuracy → fully accurate not mapped. **Flag:** Table 2 faithful denominators are internally inconsistent (GPT-4o 49/70 although only 65 sources existed; Gemini 4/8 although 18 existed).
- **R3140 Hack_2026** (otolaryngology review-writing). Cleanest definitions in the batch: fully accurate / minor metadata discrepancy / duplicate / fabricated-or-corrupted map exactly onto review outcomes. Only percentages are printed, at stated denominators (GPT-4.0 n=77; Gemini 2.0 n=46); derived counts are whole and the parts sum exactly to the denominators, so they are entered and flagged DERIVED. Retrieval explicitly none (no plugins, no browsing, no RAG).
- **R3191 Ohlsen_2026** (glenohumeral arthritis). ChatGPT (GPT-4o): 3/6 references nonexistent (fabricated); a 4th was a real article under the wrong journal name — treated as a single-field error, not counted in major (flagged in the row note for adjudication). Gemini (2.5 Pro): 0 fabricated; 5 real-but-inappropriate citations (wrong procedure comparison / did not support the claim) counted as major (misattribution). Neither arm's "appropriate/supportive" label was mapped to fully accurate, because it adds a claim-support requirement to bibliographic accuracy.
- **R3432 Lin_2025** (nephrology, Taiwan). Only one arm extracted: GPT-4 was the only model asked to provide references. 131 provided → 30 duplicates removed → 101 unique, all confirmed existing. Verification was existence-only, so the paper's "100% accurate" is NOT entered as fully accurate. GPT-4o, Gemini 1.0 Ultra and PaLM 2 have no citation data and no rows.
- **R3578 Pan_2026** (gout nursing, China; browsing ON for both models — DeepSeek Browser / Browse with Bing). DeepSeek-R1: 25 provided, 4 "false information" eliminated → 21 authentic; the 4 are entered as fabricated on the paper's own authentic/false split ("false" is not further defined — adjudicator may wish to downgrade to narrative-only). ChatGPT-4.0: 23, none reported false. No field-level accuracy assessment in either arm.
- **R4081 Eminovic_2026** (neuroradiology writing; DeepSeek-R1 vs ChatGPT-o1). The study's fabricated criterion is narrower than and different from the review definition, and "unverifiable" is reported separately, so **n_fabricated is deliberately left blank** for both arms. The (d) count is entered as n_bad_doi (a DOI resolving to a different paper is exactly the review's bad-DOI definition) and "fully verifiable" as n_fully_accurate; both are DERIVED from printed percentages (DeepSeek-R1 36.7%/22.4% of 49; ChatGPT-o1 2.6%/20.5% of 39; ChatGPT-o1 unverifiable 64.1% of 39 = 25, noted in the row).
