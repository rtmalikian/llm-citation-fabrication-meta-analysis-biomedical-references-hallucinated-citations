Locations refer to manuscript.md sections.

1. Title: identifies the report as a systematic review and meta-analysis. Title page.
2. Abstract: structured, with all PRISMA for Abstracts elements. Abstract section; registration and funding stated in it.
3. Rationale: Background, Description of the problem.
4. Objectives: Objectives, with research questions RQ1 to RQ7.
5. Eligibility criteria: Methods part 2.
6. Information sources: Methods part 4 and search-log.md, with dates and blocked sources stated.

16. Study selection results: Results, Description of studies; prisma-flow.md and diagram.
17. Study characteristics: extraction.csv and extraction-outcomes.csv (Appendix C).
18. Risk of bias in studies: Results and rob-summary.md; rob-assessments.csv.

20. Results of syntheses: Results, Synthesis of results, with pooled estimates, CIs, tau squared, I squared and prediction intervals.
21. Reporting biases: addressed under item 14 and in Limitations of the review process.
22. Certainty of evidence: summary of findings table before Background, with downgrade reasons per outcome.
23. Discussion: Discussion, with limitations of the evidence and of the review process in their own subsections.
24. Registration and protocol: title page and Declarations. No PROSPERO number was issued before analysis; the PROSPERO-ready form and OSF route are stated, and nothing is backdated.
25. Support: Declarations, funding none.
26. Competing interests: Declarations, none, fully independent.
27. Availability of data, code and other materials: Declarations, with the repository and package contents listed.
