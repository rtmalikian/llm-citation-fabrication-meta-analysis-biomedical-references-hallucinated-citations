# Highlights

- First pooled estimate of citation fabrication in LLM generated biomedical reference lists: 18.6% completely fabricated across 51 model arms and 12,828 checked references.
- Only 29.4% of generated references were fully accurate on every field checked (42 arms, 12,002 references).
- Arms with web retrieval or browsing active pooled 5.0% fabricated against 25.1% with retrieval off, on five retrieval arms.
- No included study reached low risk of bias on a tool specified before extraction; unreported verification conduct was the main weakness, and all critical outcomes are rated very low certainty.
- All screening, extraction, risk of bias and analysis files are published openly, and every pooled figure can be rebuilt from the posted code and CSVs.
