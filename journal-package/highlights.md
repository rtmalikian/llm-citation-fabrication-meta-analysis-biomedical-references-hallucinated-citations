# Highlights

- First pooled estimate of citation fabrication in LLM generated biomedical reference lists: 19.4% completely fabricated across 113 model arms and 22,461 checked references (corrected version, robustness pass 23 October 2026; first version: 18.6% across 51 arms).
- Only 37.0% of generated references were fully accurate on every field checked (58 arms, 14,224 references).
- Arms with web retrieval or browsing active pooled 5.8% fabricated against 31.0% with retrieval off, on 26 retrieval arms.
- No included study reached low risk of bias on a tool specified before extraction; unreported verification conduct was the main weakness, and all critical outcomes are rated very low certainty.
- All screening, extraction, risk of bias and analysis files are published openly, including both independent extraction passes and the discrepancy settlement log from the robustness pass, and every pooled figure can be rebuilt from the posted code and CSVs.
