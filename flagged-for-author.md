## Items the author should know about

- R0053 (Seifi 2026) per model counts are derived from printed percentages at a printed denominator of 100 per model and are flagged derived in extraction.csv. They sum to the printed pooled totals.
- R0180 (Singh 2026) prints fabricated and misrepresented as one composite (29 of 88 verified references). It enters the major inaccuracy pool only, with the composite stated.
- The deduplicated search set carried incorrect PMCID values for a number of records. Six correct full texts were recovered by DOI and title re-retrieval with title verification inside the XML. The episode is reported in llm-team-log.md.
- Bernstein 2026 counts any reference not indexed in PubMed as hallucinated. Its fabricated rate (7.13 percent) is therefore not on the same definition as studies that searched beyond PubMed, and the risk of bias assessment marks this at domain 4.
